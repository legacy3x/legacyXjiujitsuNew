The Preparation

Built to
perform.

Competing asks more of you than a regular class. This program prepares you for every part of it — body, mind, and game plan.

01

Competition Strategy

Game plans, scoring, and making smart decisions under the rules — so you know what to do before the match starts.

02

Drilling Under Fatigue

Technique that holds up when you're tired, because that's when matches are won and lost.

03

Live Rounds

Rounds with real intensity, so the pace of competition never catches you by surprise.

04

Mental Preparation

Managing nerves, staying focused, and performing under pressure when it counts.

Competition FAQ

Common
questions.

Do I have to compete?

No. Competition is completely optional. Many of our students train purely for fitness, self-defense, and personal growth with no interest in competing, and that is equally valued here.

Can beginners join?

Some prior training is recommended. If you're new to Jiu-Jitsu, start in our Adult program to build your fundamentals first.

Do I need to register with the OJA?

Yes. OJA registration is required to compete in sanctioned events. When you register, select Legacy X Jiu-Jitsu as your gym/club.

Do you compete in Gi or No-Gi?

Both. The program prepares you for Gi and No-Gi competition, from IBJJF/OJA-sanctioned events to open tournaments.

What does the preparation include?

Competition strategy, drilling under fatigue, live rounds with intensity, and the mental preparation needed to perform under pressure.
