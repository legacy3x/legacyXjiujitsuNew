Adult
Jiu-Jitsu.

Our flagship adult program covers the full spectrum of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu — from foundational movement and positional awareness through to escapes, control, and submission offense. Structured for progression, open to all experience levels.

The Curriculum

What you'll
learn.

Classes are fundamentals-focused and built for progression. Every technique is taught with the why behind it, not just the how — so what you learn on Tuesday still makes sense on Thursday, and years from now.

01

Movement

How to fall safely, move on the ground, and use your body efficiently. The foundation everything else is built on.

02

Positional Control

Understanding the key positions of Jiu-Jitsu — how to get there, how to hold them, and why position comes before submission.

03

Escapes

Staying calm under pressure and getting out of bad positions. The skills that build real confidence on and off the mat.

04

Submissions

Chokes, joint locks, and the setups that lead to them — applied with control, so your training partners stay safe.

Two Styles, One Program

Gi & No-Gi.

We teach both styles in the adult program. Each develops different and complementary skills, and training both makes you a more complete grappler.

Traditional Uniform

Gi

Gi training uses a traditional uniform (kimono) which allows for grip-based techniques.

  • Collar, sleeve, and lapel grips
  • A wide range of chokes and control options
  • Loaner gis available for trial classes
Rash Guard & Shorts

No-Gi

No-Gi training uses rash guards and shorts, focusing on wrestling-based grips and body control.

  • Wrestling-based grips
  • Body control and pace
  • Train in a rash guard and shorts
Inside a Class

What an hour
looks like.

Every adult class follows a structure designed to keep you safe and help you improve, whether it's your first class or your five-hundredth.

Step 01

Warm-Up

Proper warm-ups and Jiu-Jitsu-specific movement to get your body ready for the mat.

Step 02

Technique

The technique of the day is demonstrated and broken down step by step.

Step 03

Drilling

Controlled drilling with a partner, building the reps until the movement becomes yours.

Step 04

Sparring

Supervised live rolling. Beginners are never thrown into a live roll before they are ready.

Adult Program FAQ

Common
questions.

Do I need any experience to start?

Absolutely not. The adult program is open to all levels, and beginners with zero martial arts experience are welcome. We'll guide you through every step — from how to fall safely, to your first techniques, to your first roll.

I'm not in shape. Should I wait?

No — Jiu-Jitsu is how you get in shape. Classes start with a proper warm-up, and you're encouraged to work at your own pace. Consistency matters far more than fitness on day one.

How often should I train?

We recommend 2–3 times per week for beginners. Consistency is more important than intensity when starting out — showing up regularly and allowing your body time to recover will take you further than training every day.

Is it safe?

Yes. Safety is our top priority. Classes are structured with proper warm-ups, controlled drilling, and supervised sparring. You will never be thrown into a live roll before you are ready, and our culture of tapping early and training with control keeps everyone safe.

Do I have to compete?

Not at all. Competition is completely optional — many of our adult students train purely for fitness, self-defense, and personal growth, and that is equally valued here. If you do want to compete, our Competition program can prepare you.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Your first class is free with no commitment required. Bring a friend and you can both try a class together.
