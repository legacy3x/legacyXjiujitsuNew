Adult Jiu-Jitsu

Why Legacy X

Built on
the right
foundation.

Legacy X isn't a franchise. It's an independent academy in Woodstock built on the belief that Jiu-Jitsu changes people — and that starts with the right environment, the right teaching, and a community that shows up for each other.

01

Curriculum-driven teaching

Every class follows a structured progression — from movement fundamentals through positional control, escapes, and eventually offense. You always know where you are and what comes next.

White belt → Black belt roadmap

02

Safe, welcoming environment

A culture built on respect, hygiene, and ego-free training. New students are welcomed from day one and never thrown to the wolves. Everyone starts somewhere.

OJA Member Club

03

Real community

Small classes mean real relationships. Legacy X members know each other by name and train with purpose together — on the mats and off.
