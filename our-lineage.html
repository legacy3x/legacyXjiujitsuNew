Our
Heritage.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a living tradition passed directly from instructor to student. This unbroken chain connects Legacy X to the very origins of the art.

The Legacy Chain

Every technique taught at Legacy X traces back through a direct line of instruction spanning over a century of Jiu-Jitsu history.

Lineage 01
Mitsuyo Maeda
7th Dan Judo
Kodokan Judo

Brought Judo and Jiu-Jitsu from Japan to Brazil, laying the foundation for what would become Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.

Our commitment

What Lineage
Means to Us.

At Legacy X, lineage is not just history — it is accountability. Knowing where your techniques came from means understanding why they work.

Our instruction carries the precision and intent of every generation before us, adapted for today's training environment without losing the essence of the original art.

When you train at Legacy X, you become part of this lineage. You carry it forward every time you step on the mat, every time you teach a training partner, and every time you represent the academy.

Technique with context

Every technique we teach has a lineage. Understanding that lineage tells you not just how to do it — but why it works and where it came from.

Accountability through heritage

Lineage holds coaches accountable. It connects what happens on the mat to a tradition that is bigger than any one person or academy.

You carry it forward

Every student who trains at Legacy X becomes part of this chain. The art grows through you — on the mats, in competition, and in how you train the people around you.
