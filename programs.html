Jiu-Jitsu Over 55

Jiu-Jitsu is for every body and every age. This program is designed specifically around the needs, pace, and goals of practitioners 55 and older — prioritizing movement quality, longevity, joint health, and staying active. You'll work on the same foundational principles as our adult program, adapted to train smart and stay on the mats for years to come.

✅ Modified pacing and partner work
🧠 Focus on movement, health & longevity
💬 No prior experience required
